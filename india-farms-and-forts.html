India Farms & Forts Tour

A unique journey combining India's countryside, mountain landscapes, historic cities, royal forts and traditional culture.

Delhi → Shimla → Chandigarh → Agra → Bharatpur → Jaipur → Neemrana → Udaipur

Tour Overview

India is a land of extraordinary contrasts where modern cities, traditional villages, agricultural landscapes, royal forts and historic palaces exist side by side. This tour has been designed for travellers who want to experience this diversity.

Starting from Delhi, the journey travels into the Himalayan region around Shimla before continuing to Chandigarh and Agra. The route then moves into Rajasthan, where you experience Jaipur, Neemrana and Udaipur.

Along the way, the journey introduces travellers to traditional lifestyles, local markets, agricultural landscapes, wildlife, Mughal heritage and the magnificent architecture of Rajasthan.

What Makes This Tour Different?

Instead of concentrating only on famous monuments, this journey combines heritage sightseeing with India's countryside, mountain culture and everyday life. It is ideal for travellers looking for a more varied cultural experience.

11 Nights / 12 Days Itinerary

Suggested itinerary based on the reference route. The schedule can be customized according to your travel dates.

Day 01: Arrival in Delhi

Arrival at Delhi airport or railway station. Meet your driver and transfer to the hotel. After some rest, enjoy an optional introduction to Delhi or explore a nearby local market.

Day 02: Delhi to Shimla

After breakfast, drive towards Shimla through the changing landscapes of North India. On arrival, check into your hotel and enjoy the evening around Mall Road and the local market.

Day 03: Shimla Sightseeing

Enjoy a full day exploring Shimla and nearby Kufri. Visit important attractions and viewpoints, followed by time to explore the famous Mall Road and local markets.

Day 04: Shimla to Chandigarh

Depart for Chandigarh after breakfast. After check-in, explore the city's planned architecture and attractions such as Sukhna Lake, Rock Garden and Rose Garden.

Day 05: Chandigarh to Agra

Drive towards Agra. After arrival, check into your hotel and relax. Later you can explore local markets and traditional handicraft shops.

Day 06: Agra Sightseeing

Explore the magnificent Agra Fort and other important Mughal monuments. Later visit the Taj Mahal and enjoy the atmosphere of this historic city.

Day 07: Agra to Bharatpur

Drive towards Bharatpur with a stop at Fatehpur Sikri, the impressive historic Mughal-era complex. Continue to Bharatpur and visit Keoladeo National Park, famous for its rich birdlife.

Day 08: Bharatpur to Jaipur

After breakfast, continue towards Jaipur. Check into your hotel and spend the evening exploring the colourful bazaars of the Pink City.

Day 09: Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and other important attractions. Evening is free for shopping and exploring Jaipur's traditional markets.

Day 10: Jaipur to Neemrana

Drive towards Neemrana. Check into a heritage property and experience the atmosphere of a historic fort-palace converted into a heritage stay.

Day 11: Neemrana to Udaipur

Continue towards Udaipur. After arrival and check-in, explore selected attractions such as City Palace, Jagdish Temple and Saheliyon Ki Bari. Evening free for the local markets.

Day 12: Udaipur & Departure

Depending on your departure time, enjoy a final Udaipur sightseeing experience or optional boat ride on Lake Pichola. Transfer to Udaipur airport for your onward journey.
