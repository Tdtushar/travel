Tour Overview

Discover an extraordinary combination of royal Rajasthan, Mughal architecture, ancient temples and spiritual India. This extended private journey connects some of North India's most fascinating destinations.

Beginning in Delhi, the tour travels through Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, Pushkar and Jaipur before continuing towards Agra, Khajuraho and Varanasi.

Delhi → Bikaner → Jaisalmer → Jodhpur → Pushkar → Jaipur → Agra → Jhansi → Khajuraho → Varanasi → Delhi

Detailed Tour Itinerary

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Meet our representative at Delhi airport and transfer to your hotel. After check-in, relax or explore the lively markets of Old Delhi depending on your arrival time. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 02 – Delhi Sightseeing

Explore the historical and modern sides of Delhi. Visit Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Raj Ghat, India Gate, Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar. Return to the hotel after sightseeing.

Day 03 – Delhi to Bikaner

Travel towards Bikaner by train or private transfer according to your final travel arrangement. On arrival, check in and enjoy some free time exploring the local markets.

Day 04 – Bikaner to Jaisalmer

Explore Junagarh Fort and the historic architecture of Bikaner. Later continue towards Jaisalmer, the famous Golden City of Rajasthan.

Day 05 – Jaisalmer Sightseeing

Visit Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli and other historic attractions. In the evening, travel towards the Sam Sand Dunes for a memorable desert experience. Optional camel ride and cultural program can be arranged.

Day 06 – Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

Drive through the rural landscapes of Rajasthan towards Jodhpur. After arrival, explore the famous Blue City and visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada.

Day 07 – Jodhpur to Pushkar

Continue to Pushkar, an important pilgrimage town. Visit Pushkar Lake and Brahma Temple and enjoy time exploring the colourful local streets and markets.

Day 08 – Pushkar to Jaipur

Drive to Jaipur, the Pink City. Later visit City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal. Evening is available for shopping in Jaipur's traditional markets.

Day 09 – Jaipur to Agra

Begin the day with Amber Fort and Jal Mahal. Later drive towards Agra with a stop at Fatehpur Sikri, the historic Mughal city. Continue to Agra for overnight stay.

Day 10 – Agra Sightseeing

Early morning visit to the Taj Mahal followed by Agra Fort and Itmad-ud-Daulah. Enjoy free time in the evening for local markets and handicrafts.

Day 11 – Agra to Jhansi to Khajuraho

Travel towards Jhansi and continue to Khajuraho. Depending on timing, visit Orchha and explore its impressive palaces and temples.

Day 12 – Khajuraho Temples

Spend the day discovering the remarkable temples of Khajuraho. Explore the Western and Eastern groups and appreciate their detailed medieval stone carvings and architecture.

Day 13 – Khajuraho to Varanasi

Fly or travel towards Varanasi according to the selected arrangement. Visit Sarnath, associated with the Buddha's first sermon, before returning to Varanasi.

Day 14 – Varanasi & Ganga Experience

Experience an early morning boat ride on the Ganges and witness the historic ghats. Later explore selected temples and cultural attractions. In the evening, experience the atmosphere of the Ganga Aarti.

Day 15 – Varanasi to Delhi & Departure

Depending on your flight schedule, transfer to the airport for your flight back to Delhi or onward journey. Your memorable India heritage tour concludes here.
