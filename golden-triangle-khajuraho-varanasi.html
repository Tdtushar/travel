Golden Triangle, Khajuraho & Varanasi

Explore some of India's most celebrated destinations on one memorable journey. This tour combines the historic capital Delhi, royal Jaipur, Mughal-era Agra, the remarkable temples of Khajuraho and the spiritual city of Varanasi.

Discover India's diverse heritage through magnificent forts, palaces, ancient temples, UNESCO heritage landmarks, colourful markets and the sacred ghats of the River Ganges.

The itinerary is suitable for travellers who want to experience India's architecture, history, culture and spirituality within a well-planned private tour.

Tour Route

Delhi → Jaipur → Agra → Khajuraho → Varanasi → Delhi

Tour Highlights

A combination of royal heritage, ancient temples and spiritual India

Delhi

Explore Old and New Delhi with its historic monuments, bustling markets and impressive Mughal and colonial architecture.

Royal Jaipur

Visit Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jal Mahal and colourful traditional markets.

Taj Mahal

Discover the iconic Taj Mahal along with Agra Fort and other historic attractions.

Khajuraho Temples

Admire the intricate stone carvings and extraordinary architecture of the Khajuraho temple complexes.

Sacred Varanasi

Experience ancient ghats, temples, the Ganges and the beautiful evening Ganga Aarti.

Sarnath

Visit the important Buddhist heritage site located close to Varanasi.

09 Days Tour Itinerary

Delhi • Jaipur • Agra • Khajuraho • Varanasi

Day 01 — Arrival in Delhi

Arrive in Delhi where our representative will welcome you at the airport and transfer you to your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy some free time or explore nearby attractions and markets.

Day 02 — Delhi Sightseeing

Explore Old and New Delhi. Visit Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Chandni Chowk, India Gate, Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar. You can also explore local markets and experience the atmosphere of India's capital city.

Day 03 — Delhi to Jaipur

After breakfast, drive towards Jaipur. En route, visit Fatehpur Sikri and admire its impressive Mughal architecture. Continue to Jaipur, the famous Pink City, and enjoy an evening walk through the local markets.

Day 04 — Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore Amber Fort, Jaigarh Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and Jal Mahal. In the evening, experience traditional Rajasthani culture, cuisine, music and folk performances at an optional cultural village experience.

Day 05 — Jaipur to Agra

Drive towards Agra. En route, explore Fatehpur Sikri if not covered earlier or enjoy a stop at Sikandra, the tomb of Emperor Akbar. On arrival in Agra, check into your hotel and explore local marble handicrafts if desired.

Day 06 — Agra to Khajuraho

Begin the day with a visit to the Taj Mahal. After exploring Agra's famous monument, continue towards Khajuraho. En route, enjoy a heritage visit to Orchha with its historic temples and architecture. Arrive in Khajuraho and relax.

Day 07 — Khajuraho to Varanasi

Explore the famous Khajuraho temple complex, including the magnificent Kandariya Mahadev Temple and other historic temples. Later, continue towards Varanasi by flight or suitable onward connection. In the evening, experience the spectacular Ganga Aarti.

Day 08 — Varanasi Sightseeing

Explore the spiritual side of Varanasi. Enjoy the ghats of the Ganges and visit Sarnath, an important Buddhist heritage site. Depending on time, explore local temples, markets and museums before returning to the hotel.

Day 09 — Varanasi to Delhi & Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Varanasi airport for your flight to Delhi. From Delhi, continue with your onward international or domestic journey.
