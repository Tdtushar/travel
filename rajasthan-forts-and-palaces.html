13 Nights / 14 Days • Private Rajasthan Tour

Rajasthan Forts & Palaces

Journey through the royal heart of Rajasthan, discovering magnificent forts, grand palaces, colourful havelis, desert landscapes and the living traditions of India's Land of Kings.

Get Custom Quote WhatsApp Us
Duration 13 Nights / 14 Days
Route Delhi • Rajasthan • Agra
Travel Style Private Tour
Tour Type Customizable

Tour Overview

Explore the royal culture and architectural heritage of Rajasthan on this extended private journey. The route connects historic towns, desert cities, lakeside palaces and the famous Pink City before ending with the Taj Mahal in Agra.

The journey covers Delhi, Mandawa, Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Pushkar, Jaipur and Agra, with time for sightseeing, local markets, traditional architecture and memorable cultural experiences.

Tour Highlights

Delhi heritage & Old Delhi
Painted havelis of Mandawa
Junagarh Fort in Bikaner
Jaisalmer Fort & desert experience
Mehrangarh Fort in Jodhpur
Ranakpur temple visit
Udaipur & Lake Pichola
Pushkar Lake & Brahma Temple
Amber Fort & Jaipur heritage
Fatehpur Sikri & Taj Mahal

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01 — Arrival in Delhi

Arrival in Delhi and private transfer to your hotel. Relax after the journey and, depending on arrival time, enjoy an evening walk or explore a nearby market.

Day 02 — Delhi Sightseeing

Explore India Gate, the Parliament area, Raj Ghat, Jama Masjid, Qutub Minar and Humayun's Tomb. Evening can be spent exploring Old Delhi markets and enjoying local cuisine.

Day 03 — Delhi to Mandawa

Drive to Mandawa in the Shekhawati region. Explore its historic streets and beautifully painted havelis, famous for detailed frescoes and merchant-era architecture.

Day 04 — Mandawa to Bikaner

Continue to Bikaner and visit the impressive Junagarh Fort. Explore its courtyards, palaces and historic interiors before enjoying free time in the city.

Day 05 — Bikaner to Jaisalmer

Travel towards Jaisalmer, the Golden City. After hotel check-in, enjoy leisure time for local markets, handicrafts or relaxation.

Day 06 — Jaisalmer Sightseeing

Visit Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli and other heritage landmarks. Later experience the desert atmosphere with an optional camel ride and sunset excursion.

Day 07 — Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur, the Blue City. Visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada, followed by free time for shopping and exploring the old city.

Day 08 — Jodhpur to Udaipur

Travel towards Udaipur with a stop at Ranakpur to see its celebrated Jain temple architecture. Continue to Udaipur and relax in the evening.

Day 09 — Udaipur Sightseeing

Visit Saheliyon Ki Bari, Jagdish Temple, City Palace and Bhartiya Lok Kala Mandir. In the evening, enjoy the atmosphere of Lake Pichola and an optional boat ride.

Day 10 — Udaipur to Pushkar

Drive to Pushkar and explore Pushkar Lake, the ghats and Brahma Temple. Spend the evening browsing local markets and enjoying the town's distinctive atmosphere.

Day 11 — Pushkar to Jaipur

Continue to Jaipur, the Pink City. After check-in, enjoy leisure time for shopping, handicrafts and exploring the colourful local bazaars.

Day 12 — Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and other important landmarks. Evening is free for shopping, dining or relaxing at your hotel.

Day 13 — Jaipur to Agra

Drive to Agra with a visit to Fatehpur Sikri en route. After hotel check-in, explore the local market and enjoy an evening at leisure.

Day 14 — Agra to Delhi

Visit the Taj Mahal and selected Agra attractions in the morning. Later drive to Delhi for airport or preferred drop-off and onward travel.
