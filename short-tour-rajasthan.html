Short Tour to Rajasthan

Discover the colourful spirit of Rajasthan with a carefully planned journey through Jaipur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer and Jodhpur. This tour combines royal architecture, desert experiences, historic forts and traditional markets.

Travel in a comfortable private vehicle with a professional driver and enjoy a flexible sightseeing schedule. The itinerary can also be modified according to your travel dates, hotel preferences and sightseeing interests.

Tour Itinerary Inclusions Exclusions Enquire
Day 01

Arrival in Jaipur

Arrive in Jaipur and meet your Krishna Travelers representative/driver. Transfer to your hotel and settle in. Depending on your arrival time, you can enjoy a relaxed evening in the Pink City.

Day 02

Jaipur Sightseeing

After breakfast, begin your Jaipur sightseeing tour. Visit the magnificent Amber Fort followed by the City Palace, Jantar Mantar and the iconic Hawa Mahal.

  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Hawa Mahal
  • Local markets of Jaipur
Day 03

Jaipur to Bikaner

Drive towards Bikaner after breakfast. On arrival, check into your hotel and explore the city's historic attractions, including Junagarh Fort and other heritage landmarks.

  • Junagarh Fort
  • Lalgarh Palace area
  • Local Bikaner market
  • Optional Camel Breeding Farm visit
Day 04

Bikaner to Jaisalmer

After breakfast, continue towards Jaisalmer, the Golden City. Check in at the hotel and later explore the historic lanes and sandstone architecture of the city.

  • Jaisalmer Fort
  • Patwon Ki Haveli
  • Salim Singh Ki Haveli
  • Local market
Day 05

Jaisalmer & Sam Sand Dunes

Enjoy a desert experience around Sam Sand Dunes. Depending on your preference, camel safari and jeep safari can be arranged separately.

  • Sam Sand Dunes
  • Desert sunset
  • Optional camel safari
  • Optional jeep safari
  • Optional cultural evening/camp experience
Day 06

Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur after breakfast. In the afternoon, explore the impressive Mehrangarh Fort and visit Jaswant Thada before enjoying the atmosphere of the Blue City.

  • Mehrangarh Fort
  • Jaswant Thada
  • Blue City viewpoints
  • Local market
Day 07

Departure from Jodhpur

After breakfast, check out and proceed for your onward journey. Drop can be arranged at Jodhpur airport, railway station or another agreed location.
