Kedarnath Badrinath Do Dham Yatra

The Do Dham Yatra is a shorter pilgrimage option for travellers who want to visit the two major Himalayan shrines of Kedarnath and Badrinath without completing the full Char Dham circuit.

Uttarakhand Tourism specifically lists Kedarnath and Badrinath as the two shrines covered by the Do Dham Yatra. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Kedarnath is dedicated to Lord Shiva and is situated at an altitude of around 3,580 metres. Badrinath, dedicated to Lord Vishnu, is situated at around 3,100 metres. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

This itinerary is designed with enough time for the mountain journey, darshan and recovery after the Kedarnath trek. The exact sequence can be adjusted depending on road conditions, temple timings and accommodation availability.

6 Nights / 7 Days Detailed Itinerary

A practical Do Dham itinerary with dedicated time for Kedarnath and Badrinath Darshan.

Day 01 — Delhi → Haridwar / Rishikesh

Pickup from Delhi in the morning and proceed towards Haridwar or Rishikesh. On arrival, check into the hotel and relax. In the evening, visit Har Ki Pauri or attend Ganga Aarti, subject to local timing. Overnight stay in Haridwar / Rishikesh.

Day 02 — Haridwar → Guptkashi / Sitapur

After breakfast, begin the scenic Himalayan drive towards Guptkashi or Sitapur. The route passes through Devprayag, Rudraprayag and beautiful mountain valleys. Arrive at the hotel and relax. Overnight stay in Guptkashi / Sitapur.

Day 03 — Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Early morning proceed towards Sonprayag. From the designated transport point, continue towards Kedarnath using the available travel options. The traditional trek from the Gaurikund side is a demanding uphill mountain journey. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5} Complete Kedarnath Darshan and stay overnight near Kedarnath, subject to accommodation availability.

Day 04 — Kedarnath → Sonprayag → Guptkashi

After morning darshan and breakfast, begin the return journey from Kedarnath towards the lower transport point. Continue to Guptkashi / Sitapur and check into the hotel. The evening is kept free for rest after the Kedarnath journey. Overnight stay in Guptkashi / Sitapur.

Day 05 — Guptkashi → Badrinath

After breakfast, drive towards Badrinath through the scenic Alaknanda valley. On arrival, check into the hotel and relax. Depending on temple operations and arrival time, proceed for Badrinath Darshan in the evening. Overnight stay in Badrinath.

Day 06 — Badrinath Darshan → Rudraprayag / Srinagar

Early morning Badrinath Darshan. After breakfast, visit nearby attractions such as Mana village, subject to local conditions and available time. Later begin the return journey towards Rudraprayag or Srinagar. Overnight stay.

Day 07 — Rudraprayag / Srinagar → Haridwar → Delhi

After breakfast, continue towards Haridwar. After a short break, proceed towards Delhi. Arrival in Delhi marks the completion of your Kedarnath–Badrinath Do Dham Yatra.

Season & Registration

Kedarnath and Badrinath are high-altitude seasonal pilgrimage destinations. Uttarakhand Tourism notes that the Himalayan shrines generally remain closed during the winter and operate during the pilgrimage season. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}


Uttarakhand Tourism currently lists Char Dham Yatra 2026 registration as open through the official portal and mobile app. Registration requirements and operating rules should be checked before travel. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}
