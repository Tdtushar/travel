Tour Highlights

What you can experience

Enjoy a balanced combination of heritage, lakes, architecture, shopping and local culture.

✦

City Palace

Explore the magnificent City Palace complex overlooking Lake Pichola and discover royal architecture, courtyards and historic collections.

◉

Lake Pichola

Enjoy the peaceful surroundings of Lake Pichola with beautiful views of palaces, ghats and the Aravalli landscape.

⌂

Jagdish Temple

Visit the famous historic temple known for its detailed stone carvings and traditional Indo-Aryan architecture.

◆

Saheliyon Ki Bari

Walk through the elegant gardens, fountains and marble features built as a relaxing retreat for the royal ladies.

★

Monsoon Palace

Take in panoramic views of Udaipur and the surrounding hills from the historic Sajjangarh or Monsoon Palace.

₹

Local Markets

Shop for handicrafts, textiles, jewellery, souvenirs and traditional Rajasthani products in Udaipur's vibrant markets.

04 Day Journey

Udaipur Tour Itinerary

The itinerary can be adjusted according to your arrival time, interests and travel requirements.

DAY 01

Arrival in Udaipur & Lake Pichola

Arrive in Udaipur and meet your driver. Transfer to your hotel and relax before beginning an easy evening exploration.

  • Hotel check-in and leisure time
  • Visit Lake Pichola area
  • Explore Gangaur Ghat / nearby old city
  • Enjoy the evening atmosphere of Udaipur
DAY 02

Udaipur Heritage Sightseeing

Spend the day exploring Udaipur's most famous heritage attractions and architectural landmarks.

  • City Palace
  • Jagdish Temple
  • Saheliyon Ki Bari
  • Fateh Sagar Lake
  • Local market visit
DAY 03

Monsoon Palace & Leisure

Enjoy a relaxed day with panoramic views and optional experiences depending on your interests.

  • Visit Sajjangarh / Monsoon Palace
  • Explore scenic viewpoints
  • Optional shopping
  • Free evening for leisure or local dining
DAY 04

Departure from Udaipur

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed for your onward journey or airport/railway station transfer.

  • Breakfast and checkout
  • Optional short sightseeing based on departure time
  • Drop at airport, railway station or your next destination
