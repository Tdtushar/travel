Archaeological Study Tour

A fascinating journey through some of India's most important historical, religious and architectural destinations.

Delhi → Varanasi → Khajuraho → Orchha → Bhopal → Agra → Jaipur → Khimsar → Jaisalmer → Jodhpur → Udaipur

Tour Overview

India has a remarkable archaeological and cultural heritage, developed over thousands of years. This specially designed journey connects ancient pilgrimage centres, Buddhist monuments, temple architecture, medieval palaces, Mughal landmarks and the royal heritage of Rajasthan.

Beginning in Delhi, the journey moves towards the spiritual atmosphere of Varanasi before continuing to Khajuraho and Orchha. The route then introduces the archaeological treasures around Bhopal and Sanchi before reaching Agra and Jaipur.

The second half of the journey takes you deep into Rajasthan, covering Khimsar, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur and Udaipur. The combination of ancient monuments, historic cities, desert landscapes and living traditions makes this a distinctive cultural experience.

Ideal For

This tour is particularly suitable for travellers interested in history, archaeology, architecture, photography, Indian culture, religious heritage and traditional lifestyles.

15 Nights / 16 Days Itinerary

The following itinerary follows the archaeological and heritage route. It can be adjusted according to your travel requirements.

Day 01: Arrival in Delhi

Arrival in Delhi and assistance at the airport or railway station. Transfer to your hotel and relax. Depending on your arrival time, an optional visit to nearby attractions can be arranged. Overnight stay in Delhi.

Day 02: Delhi Heritage Sightseeing

Explore important landmarks of Delhi including Red Fort, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate and Qutub Minar. Explore the historic atmosphere of Old Delhi and its traditional markets.

Day 03: Delhi to Varanasi

Transfer to the airport for your journey to Varanasi. After arrival, check in at the hotel and visit Sarnath, an important Buddhist heritage site known for its ancient monuments and monasteries.

Day 04: Varanasi Heritage Experience

Start the morning with a boat ride on the River Ganga and observe the historic ghats and traditional rituals. Later explore important temples, lanes and cultural areas of Varanasi.

Day 05: Varanasi to Khajuraho

Continue the journey towards Khajuraho. On arrival, check in and relax. Evening at leisure to explore the local surroundings.

Day 06: Khajuraho Temple Heritage

Explore the famous temple groups of Khajuraho and study their detailed sculptures, architectural styles and historical character. Visit the Western and Eastern temple groups.

Day 07: Khajuraho to Orchha

Drive to Orchha, a fascinating medieval town on the Betwa River. Visit Orchha Fort complex, historic palaces and important temples.

Day 08: Orchha to Bhopal

Continue towards Bhopal. After arrival, visit selected museums and heritage attractions depending on available time.

Day 09: Sanchi & Udaigiri Excursion

Take an excursion to Sanchi to explore its famous Buddhist monuments. Continue to Udaigiri Caves and discover the region's ancient architectural and sculptural heritage before returning to Bhopal.

Day 10: Bhopal to Agra

Depart for Agra. After arrival, check into your hotel and relax. Depending on arrival time, explore the local heritage area or enjoy an evening cultural activity.

Day 11: Agra to Jaipur

Visit the Taj Mahal during the morning followed by Agra Fort and other important Mughal-era monuments. Later drive to Jaipur, the famous Pink City of Rajasthan.

Day 12: Jaipur Heritage Sightseeing

Explore Amber Fort, Jaigarh Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and Albert Hall Museum. Evening free for shopping in Jaipur's traditional markets.

Day 13: Jaipur to Khimsar

Drive towards Khimsar and experience a different side of Rajasthan. Enjoy the desert surroundings, rural landscape and optional wildlife or blackbuck experience depending on availability.

Day 14: Khimsar to Jaisalmer

Continue towards Jaisalmer, the Golden City. After arrival, explore the atmospheric old city and enjoy the desert landscape. Optional evening cultural program can be arranged.

Day 15: Jaisalmer to Jodhpur to Udaipur

Continue the Rajasthan heritage journey towards Jodhpur and explore the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada. Later continue towards Udaipur for your overnight stay.

Day 16: Udaipur Sightseeing & Departure

Depending on your departure schedule, enjoy selected Udaipur attractions such as City Palace and Lake Pichola. Transfer to Udaipur airport for your onward journey.
