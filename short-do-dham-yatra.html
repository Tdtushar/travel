Short Do Dham Yatra

The Short Do Dham Yatra is designed for pilgrims who have limited holiday time but want to visit both Kedarnath and Badrinath.

5 Nights / 6 Days Detailed Itinerary

A compact itinerary for travellers who want to complete Kedarnath and Badrinath in limited time.

Day 01 — Delhi → Haridwar → Guptkashi / Sitapur

Start early morning from Delhi and drive towards the Himalayan region via Haridwar and Rishikesh. Continue through Devprayag and Rudraprayag towards Guptkashi or Sitapur. Arrive in the evening and check into the hotel. Overnight stay in Guptkashi / Sitapur.

Day 02 — Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Early morning proceed towards Sonprayag. From the designated transport point, continue towards Kedarnath using the available local travel options. The traditional Kedarnath approach involves an uphill mountain journey. Complete Kedarnath Darshan and stay overnight near Kedarnath, subject to accommodation availability.

Day 03 — Kedarnath → Sonprayag → Guptkashi

After morning darshan and breakfast, begin the return journey from Kedarnath. Reach the lower transport point and continue towards Guptkashi / Sitapur. Check into the hotel and take complete rest after the Kedarnath journey. Overnight stay in Guptkashi / Sitapur.

Day 04 — Guptkashi → Badrinath

After breakfast, start the scenic drive towards Badrinath. Travel through the beautiful Alaknanda valley and Himalayan towns. Arrive at Badrinath and check into the hotel. Depending on arrival time and temple operations, proceed for Badrinath Darshan. Overnight stay in Badrinath.

Day 05 — Badrinath Darshan → Rudraprayag / Srinagar

Early morning Badrinath Darshan. After breakfast, visit nearby attractions such as Mana village, subject to local conditions and available time. Later begin the return journey towards Rudraprayag or Srinagar. Overnight stay.

Day 06 — Rudraprayag / Srinagar → Haridwar → Delhi

After breakfast, continue the journey towards Haridwar. Take a short break before proceeding towards Delhi. Arrival in Delhi marks the completion of your Short Kedarnath–Badrinath Do Dham Yatra.

Important Kedarnath Travel Information

The Short Do Dham package is compact, but the Kedarnath journey remains physically demanding.

🥾 Kedarnath Approach

The Kedarnath journey involves a substantial mountain approach from the Gaurikund side. Travellers should consider their fitness, age and medical circumstances before choosing the short itinerary.

🐴 Local Travel Options

Pony, palanquin and other locally available transport options may be available depending on current government and local arrangements. Such services are not included unless specifically mentioned in the package.

🌦 Weather Conditions

Mountain weather can change quickly. Road movement and travel schedules may be affected by rain, snowfall, landslides or other local conditions.

📋 Registration

Pilgrimage registration requirements should be completed before departure according to the current official rules. Uttarakhand Tourism currently states that Char Dham registration for 2026 is open through the official portal and mobile app. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Important Yatra Information

Plan Your Short Yatra Carefully

The Short Do Dham package saves time by reducing overnight stops, but the mountain journey itself remains demanding. Kedarnath and Badrinath are high-altitude destinations. Badrinath is around 3,100 metres and is generally open seasonally because of winter snowfall. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}


Kedarnath is also a seasonal pilgrimage destination, with Uttarakhand Tourism identifying May to October as the usual best period and noting winter closure due to heavy snowfall. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}


Actual travel dates, temple operations, road conditions, hotel availability and local transport arrangements should be confirmed before departure.
