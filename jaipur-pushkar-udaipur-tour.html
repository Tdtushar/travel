Jaipur Pushkar Udaipur Tour Overview

Experience the diverse beauty of Rajasthan with our Jaipur Pushkar Udaipur Tour. This short Rajasthan journey combines magnificent forts and palaces, spiritual heritage, colourful markets and the beautiful lakes of Udaipur.

Begin your journey in Jaipur, the famous Pink City. Explore Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and the colourful streets and markets of the city.

Continue towards Pushkar, one of Rajasthan's most important spiritual destinations. Visit the famous Brahma Temple, Pushkar Lake and explore the peaceful streets surrounding the holy lake.

Continue to Udaipur, the City of Lakes. Discover City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and the beautiful surroundings of Lake Pichola.

The complete journey can be customized according to your travel dates, hotel category, sightseeing preferences and group size.

Jaipur Pushkar Udaipur Tour Itinerary

03 Nights / 04 Days

Day 01 – Arrival in Jaipur & Sightseeing

Arrive at Jaipur Airport or railway station where our representative will welcome you and assist with your transfer to the hotel.

Begin your sightseeing with the magnificent Amber Fort. Continue to Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Albert Hall Museum and Jantar Mantar.

Later visit Jal Mahal and explore the colourful local markets of Jaipur.

You can also enjoy a traditional Rajasthani dinner and cultural programme at a local cultural village.

Overnight: Jaipur

Day 02 – Jaipur to Pushkar to Udaipur

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and begin your journey towards Pushkar.

Visit the sacred Pushkar Lake and the famous Brahma Temple. Take some time to explore the narrow streets, temples and local markets around the lake.

Continue your drive towards Udaipur, the beautiful City of Lakes.

On arrival, check in at your hotel and relax.

Overnight: Udaipur

Day 03 – Udaipur Sightseeing

After breakfast, begin your Udaipur sightseeing tour.

Visit Saheliyon Ki Bari, a beautiful historic garden, followed by Jagdish Temple.

Explore the magnificent City Palace complex and its museums, courtyards and royal architecture.

In the evening, enjoy a leisurely walk through the old streets of Udaipur towards the ghats around Lake Pichola.

You can also choose an optional boat ride on Lake Pichola, depending on weather and availability.

Overnight: Udaipur

Day 04 – Udaipur Departure

After breakfast, check out from your hotel.

Depending on your departure schedule, you can enjoy some free time for shopping or local exploration.

Our driver will transfer you to Udaipur Airport or railway station for your onward journey.
