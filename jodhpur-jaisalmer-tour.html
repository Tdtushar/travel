Jodhpur Jaisalmer Tour

Tour Overview

Experience the colourful heritage of Rajasthan on this short private journey covering the historic blue city of Jodhpur and the golden desert city of Jaisalmer.

Begin your journey from Jodhpur and travel towards Jaisalmer, with a cultural stop at Osian. Explore the ancient temples, magnificent forts, royal palaces and traditional markets of western Rajasthan.

In Jaisalmer, discover the impressive fort, historic Jain temples and the heritage village of Lodurva. Return towards Jodhpur to explore Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada and the magnificent Umaid Bhawan Palace.

This itinerary can also be customized according to your travel dates, hotel preference, sightseeing interests and number of travellers.

Day By Day

Tour Itinerary

DAY 01 Jodhpur Arrival – Jaisalmer

Arrive in Jodhpur and meet our representative/driver. Begin your drive towards Jaisalmer. En route, visit the historic temples of Osian including the Jain temple complex and Durga Mata Temple. Continue to Jaisalmer and check in at your hotel. Evening at leisure.

DAY 02 Jaisalmer Sightseeing

After breakfast, explore the fascinating heritage of Jaisalmer. Visit Jaisalmer Fort and its historic Jain temples. Continue to Lodurva, an ancient cultural site known for its temple architecture and desert heritage. Evening free to explore local markets.

DAY 03 Jaisalmer – Jodhpur

After breakfast, drive back towards Jodhpur. On arrival, visit the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort, followed by Jaswant Thada and the grand Umaid Bhawan Palace. Explore the colourful markets of the Blue City before checking into your hotel.

DAY 04 Jodhpur Departure

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel. Depending on your departure time, you may have some leisure time for local shopping. Later, transfer to Jodhpur Airport or railway station for your onward journey.
