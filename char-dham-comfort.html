Designed for travellers who want additional comfort and breathing space during the Himalayan pilgrimage.

About The Comfort Char Dham Yatra

The Comfort Char Dham Yatra is a relaxed version of the complete Uttarakhand pilgrimage. It covers the four sacred shrines of Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath while allowing more time for rest and recovery compared with a tightly scheduled itinerary.

The additional day allows the journey to be planned with more flexibility around mountain travel, hotel check-ins, temple visits and rest. This can be particularly useful for families, senior travellers who are comfortable undertaking the pilgrimage, and larger groups.

The traditional Uttarakhand Char Dham sequence begins with Yamunotri, followed by Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Delhi → Haridwar → Barkot → Yamunotri → Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Guptkashi → Kedarnath → Guptkashi → Badrinath → Rudraprayag → Haridwar → Delhi

The Four Sacred Dhams

These four Himalayan shrines form the core of the Char Dham pilgrimage in Uttarakhand. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Yamunotri

The traditional first stop of the Char Dham circuit, dedicated to Goddess Yamuna.

Gangotri

Sacred shrine dedicated to Goddess Ganga in the Himalayan town of Gangotri.

Kedarnath

High-altitude Himalayan temple dedicated to Lord Shiva and an important pilgrimage destination.

Badrinath

Sacred Badrinarayan Temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, situated beside the Alaknanda River.

Day By Day Plan

11 Nights / 12 Days Detailed Itinerary

A relaxed sample itinerary designed to provide additional breathing space during the pilgrimage.

DAY 01

Delhi → Haridwar

Begin the journey from Delhi and travel towards Haridwar. The first day is intentionally kept simple so travellers can settle into the pilgrimage.

Evening

  • Hotel check-in
  • Rest after the road journey
  • Visit Har Ki Pauri
  • Ganga Aarti if timings permit
  • Overnight stay in Haridwar
DAY 02

Haridwar → Barkot

After breakfast, begin the Himalayan road journey towards Barkot. The day focuses on reaching the Yamunotri region comfortably rather than adding unnecessary sightseeing.

Plan For The Day

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Drive towards Barkot
  • Refreshment stops en route
  • Scenic Himalayan views
  • Hotel check-in at Barkot
  • Evening rest
  • Overnight stay at Barkot
DAY 03

Barkot → Janki Chatti → Yamunotri → Barkot

Start early for Yamunotri. The vehicle proceeds towards the designated road-access point and pilgrims continue according to the current local arrangements.

Yamunotri Pilgrimage

  • Drive towards Janki Chatti
  • Continue towards Yamunotri by permitted walking / pony / palanquin arrangements
  • Yamunotri Temple Darshan
  • Time for prayer and rest
  • Return towards Janki Chatti
  • Drive back to Barkot
  • Relax at hotel
  • Overnight stay at Barkot

Uttarakhand Tourism states that the Yamunotri approach from Janki Chatti involves a steep trek and that pilgrims may use pony or palanquin options. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

DAY 04

Barkot → Uttarkashi

After breakfast, drive towards Uttarkashi. This day provides a comparatively easier transition between the Yamunotri and Gangotri portions of the pilgrimage.

Evening

  • Hotel check-in
  • Lunch / refreshments
  • Rest at hotel
  • Optional local temple visit
  • Prepare for next day's Gangotri visit
  • Overnight stay at Uttarkashi
DAY 05

Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Uttarkashi

Early morning departure towards Gangotri. The journey follows the Himalayan valleys towards the second Dham.

Gangotri Visit

  • Drive towards Gangotri
  • Gangotri Temple Darshan
  • Prayer and time near the Bhagirathi River
  • Explore the temple surroundings
  • Optional local sightseeing depending on time
  • Return to Uttarkashi
  • Relax at hotel
  • Overnight stay at Uttarkashi

Gangotri is a high-altitude pilgrimage town at approximately 3,415 metres and is accessible by motorable roads. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

DAY 06

Uttarkashi → Guptkashi

Today is a longer mountain-road travel day. The Comfort package keeps the evening free for rest after arrival.

Journey

  • Early breakfast
  • Depart Uttarkashi
  • Mountain road journey
  • Refreshment and meal breaks
  • Continue towards Guptkashi
  • Hotel check-in
  • Rest and prepare for Kedarnath
  • Overnight stay at Guptkashi
DAY 07

Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Proceed towards Sonprayag and then continue the Kedarnath pilgrimage according to the current local transport and registration arrangements.

Kedarnath Journey

  • Early departure from Guptkashi
  • Reach Sonprayag
  • Continue using the locally permitted onward transport arrangement
  • Proceed towards Kedarnath
  • Hotel / guesthouse check-in
  • Temple darshan subject to local timings
  • Evening rest
  • Overnight stay near Kedarnath

Kedarnath is a high-altitude shrine and the road journey does not reach the temple itself; the onward pilgrimage requires locally permitted arrangements. This is one reason the Comfort package avoids adding unnecessary activities around the Kedarnath portion.

DAY 08

Kedarnath — Darshan & Rest Day

This is the additional comfort day of the package. Instead of immediately combining the Kedarnath return with another long destination transfer, travellers get more time around the pilgrimage.

Day Plan

  • Morning temple darshan according to local timings
  • Prayer and spiritual time
  • Breakfast / refreshments
  • Rest and recovery
  • Begin return from Kedarnath according to local arrangements
  • Continue towards the designated lower transport point if feasible
  • Overnight location may be Kedarnath-region / Guptkashi depending on current travel conditions

The exact overnight arrangement on this day should be confirmed before booking because Kedarnath access, transport slots and weather conditions can affect the schedule.

DAY 09

Guptkashi Region → Badrinath

After adequate rest, continue towards Badrinath through the Himalayan valleys.

On The Way

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Drive towards Badrinath
  • Scenic mountain and river valley views
  • Refreshment stops
  • Hotel check-in at Badrinath
  • Temple visit according to local darshan timings
  • Overnight stay at Badrinath
DAY 10

Badrinath — Temple & Local Visit

The Comfort package provides a dedicated Badrinath day rather than immediately rushing towards the next destination.

Badrinath Experience

  • Morning Badrinath Temple Darshan
  • Time for prayer
  • Visit nearby sacred places subject to local access
  • Explore Badrinath town
  • Rest at hotel
  • Optional local sightseeing
  • Overnight stay at Badrinath

Badrinath is located at around 3,100 metres beside the Alaknanda River and remains open seasonally. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

DAY 11

Badrinath → Rudraprayag / Srinagar

After breakfast, begin the return journey through the Himalayan valleys. The route and overnight location may be adjusted according to road conditions.

Journey

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Depart Badrinath
  • Mountain sightseeing during the drive
  • Refreshment breaks
  • Continue towards Rudraprayag / Srinagar
  • Hotel check-in
  • Relaxed evening
  • Overnight stay
DAY 12

Rudraprayag / Srinagar → Haridwar → Delhi

After breakfast, begin the final journey towards Haridwar and continue onward to Delhi.

Tour Conclusion

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Drive towards Haridwar
  • Optional short stop at Haridwar
  • Continue towards Delhi
  • Drop at preferred Delhi location
  • End of Char Dham Yatra
Travel & Trek Information

Comfort Does Not Mean No Walking

The package makes the overall schedule more relaxed, but some pilgrimage sections still involve walking or locally permitted transport.

🥾 Yamunotri

The Yamunotri temple is reached from the Janki Chatti area through a steep pilgrimage route.

  • Comfortable walking shoes recommended
  • Start early
  • Pony / palanquin may be available locally
  • Carry water and personal essentials

Uttarakhand Tourism describes the Janki Chatti–Yamunotri approach as a steep trek and notes pony/palanquin options. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

🔱 Kedarnath

Kedarnath is a high-altitude pilgrimage destination and requires onward travel beyond the road-access point.

  • Plan sufficient time
  • Keep warm clothing
  • Use only authorised/local arrangements
  • Allow flexibility for weather

🏔️ High Altitude

The circuit reaches several high-altitude destinations. A slower pace can help travellers manage the long Himalayan journey.

  • Rest adequately
  • Stay hydrated
  • Avoid unnecessary exertion
  • Follow local instructions

🌦️ Weather & Roads

Himalayan weather and road conditions can change quickly.

  • Keep flexible travel dates
  • Carry rain protection
  • Keep warm clothing
  • Follow local administration advisories

Important Information

Before Booking Your Yatra

📋 Char Dham Registration

Uttarakhand Tourism currently states that Char Dham Yatra registration for 2026 is open through the official portal and mobile app. Registration requirements and procedures can change, so travellers should verify the current official requirements before departure. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

🌦️ Weather & Road Conditions

The Char Dham route is a Himalayan road journey. Weather, landslides, traffic restrictions, road conditions and local administration decisions may affect the itinerary. The actual route and overnight location may therefore be changed when necessary.

🛕 Seasonal Temple Operation

Uttarakhand Tourism notes that the high-altitude shrines generally remain closed during the winter period and open during the pilgrimage season. Exact opening and closing dates should be confirmed for your travel year. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family & Senior Travellers

The Comfort package provides a slower overall schedule, but Char Dham remains a physically demanding Himalayan pilgrimage. Travellers should consider their individual mobility and consult a medical professional where appropriate before undertaking strenuous travel.
