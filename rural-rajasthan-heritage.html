Detailed Rajasthan Itinerary

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Airport pickup and transfer to your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a relaxed evening or explore the colourful streets of Old Delhi. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 02 – Delhi to Mandawa

Drive towards Mandawa in the Shekhawati region. Explore the historic town famous for beautifully painted havelis, frescoes and traditional architecture.

Day 03 – Mandawa to Bikaner

Continue towards Bikaner. Visit Junagarh Fort and explore the old city. Experience the traditional atmosphere of one of Rajasthan's historic trading towns.

Day 04 – Bikaner to Jaisalmer

Travel through the changing desert landscape towards Jaisalmer. On arrival, check in to your hotel and enjoy an evening walk around the Golden City.

Day 05 – Jaisalmer Heritage & Desert

Visit Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli and other historic landmarks. Later travel towards the Sam Sand Dunes. Optional camel safari and traditional Rajasthani cultural evening can be arranged.

Day 06 – Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur, the famous Blue City. Visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada. Later explore the old city streets and local markets.

Day 07 – Rural Rajasthan Experience

Travel towards the countryside near Rohet. Enjoy an optional village visit and experience traditional rural life, local crafts, farms, houses and Rajasthani hospitality.

Day 08 – Rohet to Udaipur

Drive through the Aravalli landscape towards Udaipur. En route, optional stops at traditional villages and scenic locations can be arranged.

Day 09 – Udaipur Heritage

Explore Udaipur including City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and Lake Pichola. Enjoy an optional evening boat ride.

Day 10 – Udaipur to Pushkar

Continue towards Pushkar. Visit Pushkar Lake and Brahma Temple and explore the colourful lanes, markets and traditional shops.

Day 11 – Pushkar to Jaipur & Departure

Drive towards Jaipur. Depending on your departure schedule, visit a selected Jaipur attraction or enjoy some shopping before your onward journey.
