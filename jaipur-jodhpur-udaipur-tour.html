Jaipur Jodhpur Udaipur Tour Overview

Discover three of Rajasthan's most fascinating destinations with our Jaipur Jodhpur Udaipur Tour. This journey combines royal palaces, magnificent forts, colourful markets, traditional culture and the romantic lakes of Udaipur.

Begin your journey in Jaipur, the famous Pink City, where you can explore Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and the colourful bazaars of the old city.

Continue to Jodhpur, known as the Blue City, and discover the impressive Mehrangarh Fort, Umaid Bhawan Palace and Jaswant Thada.

Your Rajasthan experience concludes in Udaipur, the City of Lakes. Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and enjoy the beautiful surroundings of Lake Pichola.

The itinerary can be customized according to your travel dates, hotel category, sightseeing interests and group size.

Jaipur Jodhpur Udaipur Tour Itinerary

07 Nights / 08 Days

Day 01 – Arrival in Jaipur & Sightseeing

Arrive at Jaipur Airport or railway station where our representative will welcome you and transfer you to the hotel.

Later begin Jaipur sightseeing with Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, City Palace and Albert Hall Museum.

Evening is free for exploring local markets and enjoying traditional Rajasthani cuisine.

Overnight: Jaipur

Day 02 – Jaipur Sightseeing

After breakfast, visit the magnificent Amber Fort situated in the Aravalli hills.

Continue to Jaigarh Fort and explore its historic architecture and famous cannon.

Later visit Jal Mahal and enjoy free time for shopping in Jaipur's colourful markets.

In the evening, you may experience traditional Rajasthani food, music and folk performances at a cultural village.

Overnight: Jaipur

Day 03 – Jaipur to Jodhpur

After breakfast, check out and drive towards Jodhpur, the famous Blue City of Rajasthan.

On arrival, check in to your hotel and relax.

Later visit Mehrangarh Fort, one of India's most impressive historic forts, followed by Umaid Bhawan Palace and Jaswant Thada.

Evening is free to explore the old city and taste authentic Jodhpuri cuisine.

Overnight: Jodhpur

Day 04 – Jodhpur to Udaipur via Ranakpur

After breakfast, proceed towards Udaipur.

En route, visit the beautiful Ranakpur Jain Temple, famous for its detailed marble architecture and beautifully carved pillars.

Continue your drive to Udaipur and check in at your hotel.

Evening at leisure.

Overnight: Udaipur

Day 05 – Udaipur Sightseeing

After breakfast, start your full-day sightseeing tour of Udaipur.

Visit Saheliyon Ki Bari, Jagdish Temple and the magnificent City Palace complex.

Explore the royal palaces, courtyards, museums and beautiful views over Lake Pichola.

Evening is perfect for enjoying the sunset and relaxing beside the lake.

Overnight: Udaipur

Day 06 – Udaipur Cultural Experience

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed day in Udaipur.

Explore local handicraft markets, traditional artwork and the old streets around the historic city.

You may also choose an optional boat ride on Lake Pichola depending on weather and availability.

Overnight: Udaipur

Day 07 – Udaipur Leisure Day

Enjoy a relaxed morning at your hotel or explore additional attractions according to your interests.

This day can be customized for shopping, photography, cultural experiences or leisure.

Overnight: Udaipur

Day 08 – Udaipur Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel.

Our driver will transfer you to Udaipur Airport or railway station according to your departure schedule.
