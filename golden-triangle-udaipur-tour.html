Golden Triangle & Udaipur

This 7 Nights / 8 Days journey combines India's iconic Golden Triangle with the beautiful city of Udaipur. Explore the historic streets of Delhi, the magnificent Taj Mahal in Agra, the royal heritage of Jaipur and the peaceful lakes and palaces of Udaipur.

The tour is designed for travellers who want to experience India's history, architecture, culture, food and royal traditions while travelling comfortably by private vehicle.

From Mughal monuments to Rajput forts and the romantic atmosphere of Lake Pichola, every destination brings a different side of India to your journey.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

07 Nights / 08 Days

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Arrive at Delhi International Airport where our representative will meet you and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in, relax and enjoy some free time. In the evening, you can explore the lively markets of Old Delhi.

Day 02 – Delhi Sightseeing

After breakfast, enjoy a full day of Delhi sightseeing. Explore attractions such as Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Qutub Minar, India Gate, President House and Lotus Temple. Evening free for shopping and local exploration.

Day 03 – Delhi to Agra

After breakfast, drive to Agra. On arrival, check into your hotel and later visit the magnificent Taj Mahal and historic Agra Fort. Evening can be spent exploring local markets and traditional handicrafts.

Day 04 – Agra to Jaipur via Fatehpur Sikri

Enjoy an early visit to the Taj Mahal if desired. After breakfast, drive towards Jaipur. En-route stop at Fatehpur Sikri to explore its impressive Mughal architecture. Continue to Jaipur and check in at your hotel.

Day 05 – Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore the Pink City with visits to Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and Jal Mahal. In the evening, enjoy shopping in Jaipur's famous bazaars or experience traditional Rajasthani culture and cuisine.

Day 06 – Jaipur to Udaipur

After breakfast, depart for Udaipur. The journey takes you through the landscapes of Rajasthan. On arrival, check in at your hotel and relax. If time permits, enjoy an evening boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 07 – Udaipur Sightseeing

Spend the day exploring Udaipur. Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and the old city. Later explore local markets and enjoy the beautiful atmosphere around the lakes.

Day 08 – Udaipur to Delhi / Departure

After breakfast, complete your final transfer according to your onward travel arrangements. Your Golden Triangle with Udaipur tour concludes with wonderful memories of India's heritage and royal cities.
