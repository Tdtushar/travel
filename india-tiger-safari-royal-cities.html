India Tiger Safari & Royal Cities Tour

Experience some of India's most memorable destinations on one journey. This private tour combines the historic streets and monuments of Delhi, the timeless beauty of Agra, the wildlife of Ranthambore and the royal heritage of Jaipur.

Travel between destinations in a comfortable private vehicle with an experienced driver. The itinerary can be adjusted according to your travel dates, hotel category, sightseeing preferences and safari requirements.

Delhi Agra Ranthambore Jaipur Delhi
Tour Itinerary Inclusions Exclusions Enquire
Day 01

Arrival in Delhi

Arrive in Delhi and meet your Krishna Travelers representative or driver. Transfer to your hotel and relax. If your arrival time permits, you can begin with a short introduction to the city.

  • Airport / railway station pickup
  • Hotel check-in
  • Optional evening visit to a nearby attraction or market
  • Overnight stay in Delhi
Day 02

Delhi Sightseeing

Spend the day discovering Old and New Delhi. Explore historic monuments, important landmarks and the colourful markets of the capital.

  • Red Fort
  • Jama Masjid and Old Delhi
  • Chandni Chowk
  • India Gate
  • Humayun's Tomb
  • Qutub Minar
  • Lotus Temple or another attraction depending on timing
Day 03

Delhi to Agra & Taj Mahal

After breakfast, drive to Agra. On arrival, check in and begin sightseeing. Visit the impressive Agra Fort and explore the historic Mughal heritage of the city.

  • Drive Delhi to Agra
  • Agra Fort
  • Itmad-ud-Daulah or Mehtab Bagh as time permits
  • Taj Mahal visit according to opening day and timing
  • Overnight stay in Agra
Day 04

Agra to Ranthambore

After breakfast, continue towards Ranthambore. The route can include a heritage stop such as Fatehpur Sikri depending on the agreed itinerary and available time.

  • Agra to Ranthambore transfer
  • Optional Fatehpur Sikri visit
  • Check-in at jungle resort / hotel
  • Evening leisure
Day 05

Ranthambore Tiger Safari

Enjoy a wildlife experience in Ranthambore National Park. Safari permits and zones are subject to park rules and availability. Early morning and afternoon safari options can be discussed when planning the trip.

  • Morning wildlife safari
  • Leisure time at the resort
  • Optional local sightseeing
  • Second safari subject to availability
Day 06

Ranthambore to Jaipur

After breakfast, drive towards Jaipur, Rajasthan's famous Pink City. On arrival, check in and enjoy a relaxed evening or explore a local market.

  • Ranthambore to Jaipur transfer
  • Hotel check-in
  • Optional evening market visit
  • Overnight stay in Jaipur
Day 07

Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore Jaipur's royal architecture and famous landmarks. The day combines forts, palaces, observatory architecture and colourful city markets.

  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Hawa Mahal
  • Jal Mahal photo stop
  • Jaipur local shopping
Day 08

Jaipur to Delhi & Departure

After breakfast, begin the journey back to Delhi. Depending on your departure time, the vehicle can drop you at Delhi airport, railway station or another agreed location.

  • Jaipur to Delhi transfer
  • Airport / railway station drop
  • End of tour
