Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Airport pickup and transfer to your hotel. After check-in and rest, enjoy an optional evening exploration of Old Delhi and its colourful markets. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 02 – Delhi Sightseeing

Explore Old and New Delhi including Red Fort, Jama Masjid, India Gate, Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar. Evening at leisure. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 03 – Delhi to Jaipur

Drive to Jaipur, the Pink City. After check-in, visit City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal, followed by free time in the local markets.

Day 04 – Jaipur & Samode

Visit Amber Fort and continue towards Samode. Enjoy the peaceful village surroundings and experience local Rajasthani culture.

Day 05 – Samode to Mandawa

Drive to Mandawa, famous for its beautifully painted havelis. Explore the old town, frescoes and traditional architecture.

Day 06 – Mandawa to Bikaner

Continue to Bikaner. Visit Junagarh Fort and other heritage attractions, with optional local market exploration.

Day 07 – Bikaner to Manvar

Drive towards the desert region of Rajasthan. Relax at your stay and enjoy the changing landscape and local surroundings.

Day 08 – Manvar to Rohet

Continue to Rohet and experience rural Rajasthan. An optional jeep safari through nearby villages can be arranged.

Day 09 – Rohet to Kumbhalgarh

Drive to Kumbhalgarh and explore the magnificent Kumbhalgarh Fort and surrounding Aravalli landscape.

Day 10 – Kumbhalgarh to Udaipur

Proceed to Udaipur, the City of Lakes. Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple and other cultural attractions.

Day 11 – Udaipur Exploration

Enjoy a relaxed day exploring Udaipur. Optional visits include Eklingji and Nagda temples, followed by a boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 12 – Udaipur to Dungarpur

Travel to Dungarpur and discover its palaces, temples, museum and local markets.

Day 13 – Dungarpur to Ahmedabad

Continue to Ahmedabad. Explore selected heritage and cultural attractions such as Gandhi Ashram and the historic old city.

Day 14 – Ahmedabad to Mumbai

Free time for local shopping before onward travel to Mumbai. On arrival, explore selected Mumbai highlights depending on your flight timing.

Day 15 – Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Mumbai airport for your onward flight. Your Rajasthan heritage journey ends with Krishna Travelers India.
