Jaipur, Bikaner & Pushkar Tour

Discover the colourful heritage of Rajasthan on a private journey connecting Jaipur, Bikaner and Pushkar.

08 Days Tour Itinerary

Jaipur • Bikaner • Pushkar

Day 01 — Arrival in Jaipur

Arrive in Jaipur and begin your Rajasthan experience with a visit to Amber Fort. Explore the historic fort complex and enjoy the views surrounding the Aravalli hills.

Later visit Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Albert Hall Museum and Jantar Mantar. In the evening, visit Jal Mahal and optionally enjoy a traditional Rajasthani cultural evening at Chokhi Dhani.

Overnight stay in Jaipur.

Day 02 — Jaipur to Bikaner

After breakfast, drive towards Bikaner. On arrival, check into your hotel and explore the historic city.

Visit the magnificent Junagarh Fort and discover the unique architectural heritage of Bikaner.

Overnight stay in Bikaner.

Day 03 — Bikaner Sightseeing

Explore more of Bikaner's royal heritage. Visit historic havelis, local markets and important cultural attractions.

You may also visit the famous Karni Mata Temple at Deshnok, known for its unusual temple traditions.

Overnight stay in Bikaner.

Day 04 — Bikaner to Pushkar

After breakfast, drive towards Pushkar. On arrival, explore the sacred Pushkar Lake and surrounding ghats.

Visit the famous Brahma Temple and enjoy an evening walk through the colourful local bazaar.

Overnight stay in Pushkar.

Day 05 — Pushkar Exploration

Spend the day exploring Pushkar at a relaxed pace. Visit its temples, lake and traditional markets.

Optional activities can include visiting Savitri Temple or enjoying a peaceful sunset around Pushkar Lake.

Overnight stay in Pushkar.

Day 06 — Pushkar to Jaipur

After breakfast, drive back towards Jaipur. Enjoy free time after arrival for shopping, local sightseeing or relaxation.

Jaipur is famous for jewellery, textiles, handicrafts, blue pottery and traditional Rajasthani products.

Overnight stay in Jaipur.

Day 07 — Jaipur Leisure & Shopping

Enjoy a flexible day in Jaipur. You can revisit your favourite attraction, explore local bazaars or simply relax at your hotel.

Our team can customize this day according to your interests and travel schedule.

Overnight stay in Jaipur.

Day 08 — Jaipur Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Jaipur airport, railway station or your preferred departure point for your onward journey.

Your Rajasthan holiday comes to an end with beautiful memories of Jaipur, Bikaner and Pushkar.
