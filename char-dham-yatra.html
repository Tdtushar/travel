STANDARD ITINERARY

Char Dham Yatra At A Glance

Day 01 — Delhi / Haridwar

Arrival at Haridwar, hotel check-in and evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri.

Day 02 — Haridwar to Barkot

Drive towards Barkot through the Himalayan foothills. Evening rest at hotel.

Day 03 — Yamunotri

Transfer towards Janki Chatti followed by the Yamunotri pilgrimage and return to Barkot.

Day 04 — Barkot to Uttarkashi

Drive to Uttarkashi and visit the local temple area.

Day 05 — Gangotri

Excursion to Gangotri Dham and return to Uttarkashi.

Day 06 — Uttarkashi to Guptkashi

Scenic Himalayan drive towards Guptkashi with overnight stay.

Day 07 — Kedarnath

Transfer towards Sonprayag/Gaurikund and continue the Kedarnath pilgrimage.

Day 08 — Kedarnath Return

Morning darshan and return towards Guptkashi/Sitapur as per route conditions.

Day 09 — Guptkashi to Badrinath

Drive through the Himalayan valleys towards Badrinath.

Day 10 — Badrinath

Badrinath Temple darshan and optional Mana Village visit depending on conditions.

Day 11 — Badrinath to Rudraprayag

Begin return journey through the Himalayan region.

Day 12 — Rudraprayag to Haridwar

Drive towards Haridwar and complete the pilgrimage journey.
