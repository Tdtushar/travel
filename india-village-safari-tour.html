Village Safari Tour of India

Experience the real colours of rural Rajasthan

Explore Rural India Beyond the Cities

Step away from the usual tourist routes and experience the traditional side of India through our Village Safari Tour. This journey combines rural communities, heritage properties, ancient temples, forts, local markets and royal cities.

From the colourful villages of Rajasthan to the historic streets of Jaipur, Udaipur, Delhi and Agra, this itinerary offers an opportunity to experience India's diverse culture in a more personal way.

Enjoy village excursions, local interactions, traditional architecture, countryside landscapes and the heritage of Rajasthan while travelling comfortably in a private vehicle.

15 Days Village Safari Itinerary

A carefully planned journey through rural and royal India

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Arrive in Delhi and meet our representative. Transfer to your hotel and relax. Later, explore a local market or enjoy some free time in India's capital city.

Day 02 – Delhi to Alsisar

After breakfast, drive towards Alsisar in Rajasthan. En-route, explore attractions around Bikaner and Deshnok. Continue to Alsisar and experience the local surroundings.

Day 03 – Alsisar to Gajner

Travel towards Gajner. After check-in, explore the heritage surroundings, temples, lake area and local attractions. Evening at leisure.

Day 04 – Gajner to Osian

Drive towards Osian, an ancient temple town of Rajasthan. Visit important temples and enjoy an evening walk through the local market.

Day 05 – Osian to Rohetgarh

Continue towards Rohetgarh. Check in and explore the heritage surroundings. Visit the historic Rohetgarh Fort and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.

Day 06 – Rohetgarh to Kumbhalgarh

Drive to Kumbhalgarh and explore one of Rajasthan's most impressive fort complexes. Enjoy the surrounding Aravalli landscapes.

Day 07 – Kumbhalgarh to Udaipur

Visit the historic Haldighati region before continuing towards Udaipur. Check in and enjoy free time in the evening.

Day 08 – Udaipur Sightseeing

Explore Udaipur including Saheliyon Ki Bari, Jagdish Temple, local cultural attractions and the old city. In the evening, enjoy a relaxing boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 09 – Udaipur to Deogarh

Travel to Deogarh. After check-in, enjoy a village excursion and experience the surrounding countryside, local culture and traditional lifestyle.

Day 10 – Deogarh to Samode

Continue to Samode, known for its heritage atmosphere and traditional Rajasthan setting. Explore Samode Fort and surrounding village areas.

Day 11 – Samode to Jaipur

Enjoy a relaxed morning at Samode. Later, drive to Jaipur, the famous Pink City of Rajasthan. Check in and spend the evening at leisure.

Day 12 – Jaipur Sightseeing

Full-day Jaipur sightseeing covering Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and other important attractions. Evening free for shopping in Jaipur's colourful markets.

Day 13 – Jaipur to Agra

Enjoy a relaxed morning before driving to Agra. Arrive in the evening, check in at the hotel and relax.

Day 14 – Agra to Delhi

Visit the magnificent Taj Mahal in the morning. Later, explore Agra Fort and other heritage attractions before continuing towards Delhi.

Day 15 – Delhi Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Delhi airport for your onward flight. Your Village Safari journey comes to an end with memorable experiences of rural and royal India.
