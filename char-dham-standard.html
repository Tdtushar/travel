About This Char Dham Yatra

The Standard Char Dham Yatra is designed for families, groups and devotees who want to visit all four major pilgrimage shrines of Uttarakhand in one organised journey.

The traditional Char Dham circuit covers Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. Uttarakhand Tourism describes the circuit as a clockwise pilgrimage beginning at Yamunotri and continuing through Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.

The journey combines long Himalayan road drives, temple visits, mountain scenery and walking/trekking sections. The exact road schedule may be adjusted depending on weather, traffic, local restrictions and pilgrimage conditions.

Delhi → Haridwar → Barkot → Yamunotri → Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Guptkashi → Kedarnath → Badrinath → Rudraprayag → Haridwar → Delhi

The Four Sacred Dhams

The four shrines form the central pilgrimage circuit of this journey. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Yamunotri

The sacred shrine dedicated to Goddess Yamuna and the traditional starting point of the Char Dham circuit.

Gangotri

The revered shrine dedicated to Goddess Ganga, surrounded by the Himalayan landscape of Uttarkashi district.

Kedarnath

A high-altitude Himalayan temple dedicated to Lord Shiva and one of India's revered Jyotirlinga sites.

Badrinath

The sacred Badrinarayan Temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, located beside the Alaknanda River.

Day By Day Plan

10 Nights / 11 Days Detailed Itinerary

The following is a sample private-tour itinerary. Driving times and overnight locations can change according to road and weather conditions.

DAY 01

Delhi → Haridwar

Begin your Char Dham journey from Delhi in the morning and drive towards Haridwar. After arrival, check into your hotel and relax.

Evening Experience

  • Visit Har Ki Pauri
  • Attend Ganga Aarti if timings permit
  • Explore the surrounding ghats and market
  • Overnight stay in Haridwar
DAY 02

Haridwar → Barkot

After breakfast, depart Haridwar and travel towards Barkot through the Himalayan foothills.

En Route

  • Mountain scenery
  • Local roadside stops
  • Continue towards Barkot
  • Hotel check-in and rest
  • Overnight stay at Barkot
DAY 03

Barkot → Janki Chatti → Yamunotri → Barkot

Start early for the first Dham of the journey. Drive towards Janki Chatti and continue the pilgrimage to Yamunotri.

Yamunotri Pilgrimage

  • Proceed from Janki Chatti towards Yamunotri
  • Walking / pony / palki options may be available locally
  • Yamunotri Temple Darshan
  • Spend time at the sacred surroundings
  • Return towards Janki Chatti
  • Drive back to Barkot
  • Overnight stay at Barkot

The official Uttarakhand Tourism information describes the Yamunotri approach from Janki Chatti as a steep walking route and notes pony/palanquin options for pilgrims. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

DAY 04

Barkot → Uttarkashi

After breakfast, depart Barkot for Uttarkashi. The journey passes through scenic mountain roads and valleys.

Evening

  • Hotel check-in at Uttarkashi
  • Visit local temple area if time permits
  • Rest and prepare for Gangotri
  • Overnight stay at Uttarkashi
DAY 05

Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Uttarkashi

Early morning departure for Gangotri, the second Dham of the pilgrimage. The route follows the Bhagirathi valley towards the shrine.

Gangotri Visit

  • Gangotri Temple Darshan
  • Spend time near the Bhagirathi River
  • Explore the temple surroundings
  • Return towards Uttarkashi
  • Overnight stay at Uttarkashi
DAY 06

Uttarkashi → Guptkashi

Today involves a longer Himalayan road journey towards the Kedarnath region. Start early after breakfast.

Journey

  • Travel through mountain valleys
  • En-route meal / refreshment stops
  • Continue towards Guptkashi
  • Hotel check-in
  • Rest and prepare for Kedarnath
  • Overnight stay at Guptkashi
DAY 07

Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

This is one of the most important days of the pilgrimage. Travel towards Sonprayag and proceed according to the current local transport and pilgrimage arrangements.

Kedarnath Journey

  • Guptkashi to Sonprayag
  • Proceed towards the designated trek/transport point
  • Continue towards Kedarnath
  • Hotel / guesthouse check-in
  • Evening temple darshan or aarti subject to local timings
  • Overnight stay near Kedarnath

Kedarnath is situated at around 3,580 metres and the road journey does not reach the temple itself; pilgrims must use the permitted onward travel arrangements. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

DAY 08

Kedarnath → Sonprayag → Guptkashi

After morning darshan, begin the return journey from Kedarnath according to the locally permitted route and transport arrangement.

Return Journey

  • Morning temple visit / darshan
  • Begin descent from Kedarnath
  • Reach the designated lower transport point
  • Drive towards Guptkashi
  • Check into hotel and rest
  • Overnight stay at Guptkashi
DAY 09

Guptkashi → Badrinath

After breakfast, depart for Badrinath, the fourth and final major Dham of this itinerary.

On Arrival

  • Hotel check-in
  • Visit Badrinath Temple according to local darshan timings
  • Explore the temple surroundings
  • Visit Tapt Kund area where permitted
  • Overnight stay at Badrinath

Badrinath is located at approximately 3,100 metres in the Garhwal Himalayas beside the Alaknanda River. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

DAY 10

Badrinath → Rudraprayag / Srinagar

After morning activities and darshan, begin the return journey through the Himalayan valleys.

Possible En-Route Experiences

  • Mountain viewpoints
  • River valley scenery
  • Local villages and roadside stops
  • Continue towards Rudraprayag / Srinagar
  • Overnight stay
DAY 11

Rudraprayag / Srinagar → Haridwar → Delhi

After breakfast, begin the final road journey towards Haridwar and continue onward to Delhi.

Tour Conclusion

  • Drive towards Haridwar
  • Optional short break at Haridwar
  • Continue towards Delhi
  • Drop-off at your preferred location

Your Standard Char Dham Yatra concludes with the completion of the four major pilgrimage destinations.

Travel Information

Important Trek & Mountain Travel Information

Char Dham is a Himalayan pilgrimage and some sections require significant walking or locally permitted transport arrangements.

🥾 Yamunotri

The temple approach from Janki Chatti involves a steep walking section. Local pony and palanquin options may be available depending on current arrangements. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

  • Start early
  • Carry drinking water
  • Wear comfortable footwear
  • Allow adequate time

🔱 Kedarnath

Kedarnath is a high-altitude pilgrimage destination. The onward journey from the road-access area involves walking or other locally permitted options.

  • Start according to local schedule
  • Carry warm clothing
  • Keep essential medicines
  • Allow flexibility for weather

🏔️ High Altitude

Several destinations are located at significant elevations. Travellers should pace themselves and follow local safety instructions.

  • Take adequate rest
  • Avoid unnecessary exertion
  • Stay hydrated
  • Inform your travel group of any difficulty

🌦️ Weather & Roads

Mountain weather can change quickly and road conditions may affect the planned schedule.

  • Keep buffer time
  • Carry rain protection
  • Follow local advisories
  • Itinerary may change if required for safety
