About This Yatra

Kedarnath Special Yatra is designed for devotees who specifically want to visit the sacred Kedarnath Dham without covering the complete Char Dham circuit.

The journey combines road travel through the Himalayan valleys with the pilgrimage from Sonprayag/Gaurikund towards Kedarnath Temple.

Our package can be customised according to your travel dates, accommodation preference, vehicle requirement and pilgrimage needs.

  • Dedicated Kedarnath pilgrimage
  • Private vehicle from Delhi
  • Hotel stay at suitable en-route locations
  • Assistance during the yatra
  • Flexible travel dates
  • Family and group friendly

ॐ Kedarnath Dham

Kedarnath Temple is one of the most revered Shiva pilgrimage destinations in the Himalayas.

Location

Rudraprayag District, Uttarakhand


Altitude

Approx. 3,580 metres


Trek

Gaurikund to Kedarnath is a demanding Himalayan pilgrimage route.

Yatra Route

Delhi to Kedarnath and back

Delhi → Haridwar → Guptkashi / Sitapur → Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath → Delhi

Detailed 4 Days Itinerary

Suggested Kedarnath Special Yatra plan

Day 01 – Delhi → Haridwar → Guptkashi / Sitapur

Early morning departure from Delhi towards Uttarakhand. Travel through Haridwar, Rishikesh and the Himalayan roads towards Guptkashi or Sitapur. Check in at the hotel and rest after the long journey.

Overnight: Guptkashi / Sitapur

Day 02 – Guptkashi / Sitapur → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Early morning transfer towards Sonprayag. From the designated road-head area, proceed towards Gaurikund and begin the Kedarnath pilgrimage.

Depending on availability, weather and official arrangements, pilgrims may choose the permitted trekking or other available assistance options.

Reach Kedarnath and attend darshan according to the temple schedule and crowd conditions.

Overnight: Kedarnath

Day 03 – Kedarnath → Gaurikund → Guptkashi / Sitapur

Morning darshan and prayers at Kedarnath Temple. After darshan, begin the return journey towards Gaurikund.

Continue by road towards Guptkashi or Sitapur. Check in and relax after the pilgrimage.

Overnight: Guptkashi / Sitapur

Day 04 – Guptkashi / Sitapur → Haridwar → Delhi

After breakfast, start the return journey towards Haridwar/Rishikesh. Continue onward to Delhi with suitable meal and rest stops on the way.

Tour concludes with wonderful memories of the Kedarnath Dham pilgrimage.

Tour Ends: Delhi

Important Kedarnath Travel Information

Plan your pilgrimage carefully

Kedarnath is a high-altitude Himalayan pilgrimage. The final road journey does not reach the temple itself; pilgrims proceed towards the trek route from the Gaurikund side.

The official Uttarakhand Tourism page identifies Gaurikund as the starting point of the Kedarnath trek and provides road access information for the region.

⚠ Important

Yatra registration, route permissions, weather conditions, temple timings, trek timings and available transport/assistance can change. Travellers should follow the latest instructions issued by the Uttarakhand authorities before and during the journey.
