Tour Overview

Experience India's famous Golden Triangle together with some of Rajasthan's most fascinating royal cities. This private journey brings together historic monuments, magnificent forts, colourful bazaars, traditional culture and unforgettable landscapes.

Starting in Delhi, the journey continues to Agra and Jaipur before exploring the heritage cities of Jodhpur, Udaipur and Pushkar. The itinerary can be customized according to your travel dates, hotel category and interests.

Delhi → Agra → Jaipur → Jodhpur → Udaipur → Pushkar → Delhi

Detailed Tour Itinerary

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Meet our representative at Delhi airport and transfer to your hotel. After check-in, relax or enjoy an evening introduction to Delhi depending on your arrival time.

Day 02 – Delhi Sightseeing

Explore Old and New Delhi including Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Raj Ghat, India Gate, Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar. Return to the hotel after sightseeing.

Day 03 – Delhi to Agra

Drive to Agra. On arrival visit Agra Fort and explore the historic surroundings. Evening at leisure or optional local market visit.

Day 04 – Agra to Jaipur

Enjoy an early morning visit to the Taj Mahal. Later drive towards Jaipur with a stop at Fatehpur Sikri, the impressive former Mughal capital.

Day 05 – Jaipur Heritage Sightseeing

Explore Amber Fort, Jal Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal. Evening is available for shopping in Jaipur's colourful markets.

Day 06 – Jaipur to Jodhpur

Drive towards Jodhpur through the Rajasthan countryside. On arrival explore the old city and its famous blue-painted houses.

Day 07 – Jodhpur Sightseeing

Visit the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada. Later explore the Clock Tower market and old city streets.

Day 08 – Jodhpur to Udaipur

Travel towards Udaipur through the Aravalli landscape. Optional sightseeing stops can be arranged en route. Arrive in Udaipur and relax.

Day 09 – Udaipur Sightseeing

Explore City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and the beautiful lakes of Udaipur. Enjoy an optional evening boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 10 – Udaipur to Pushkar

Drive to Pushkar. Visit Pushkar Lake and Brahma Temple and explore the colourful local markets and narrow streets.

Day 11 – Pushkar to Jaipur

Return towards Jaipur. Enjoy free time for shopping, handicrafts and local Rajasthani products.

Day 12 – Jaipur to Delhi

After breakfast, drive back to Delhi. Depending on your schedule, enjoy some free time in Delhi for shopping or additional sightseeing.

Day 13 – Departure from Delhi

Transfer to Delhi airport or railway station according to your onward travel schedule. Your Golden Triangle and Royal Cities journey ends here.
