Jaipur Jodhpur Tour Overview

Experience the royal charm of Rajasthan with our Jaipur Jodhpur Tour. This carefully planned short journey combines the historic grandeur of Jaipur with the magnificent forts and royal heritage of Jodhpur.

Explore Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and the colourful markets of Jaipur before travelling towards Jodhpur, the famous Blue City of Rajasthan.

In Jodhpur, discover the impressive Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada and the magnificent Umaid Bhawan Palace. The tour can also be customized according to your travel dates, hotel category and sightseeing preferences.

Whether you are travelling as a couple, family, group or international traveller, Krishna Travelers India can arrange a comfortable private Rajasthan experience.

Tour Highlights

Royal Jaipur

Visit Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and other iconic attractions of Jaipur.

Blue City Jodhpur

Explore the historic lanes and blue-painted houses surrounding the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort.

Rajasthan Culture

Enjoy traditional food, colourful markets, handicrafts and the vibrant culture of Rajasthan.

Jaipur Jodhpur Tour Itinerary

03 Nights / 04 Days

Day 01 – Arrival in Jaipur & Sightseeing

Arrive in Jaipur where our representative will welcome you and assist you with your transfer to the hotel.

Begin sightseeing with Amber Fort and enjoy the spectacular views of the surrounding Aravalli hills. Continue towards Jaigarh Fort and explore its impressive historic architecture.

Later visit Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Albert Hall Museum. In the evening you can experience traditional Rajasthani culture and dinner at a cultural village-style venue such as Chokhi Dhani.

Overnight: Jaipur

Day 02 – Jaipur to Jodhpur

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive towards Jodhpur, the famous Blue City of Rajasthan.

Enjoy the changing landscapes of Rajasthan during the journey. On arrival in Jodhpur, check in at your hotel and relax.

Evening is free to explore the local surroundings or visit the colourful markets of the old city.

Overnight: Jodhpur

Day 03 – Jodhpur Sightseeing

After breakfast, start your Jodhpur sightseeing tour with the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort, one of Rajasthan's most impressive hill forts.

Continue to Jaswant Thada, a beautiful marble memorial surrounded by peaceful gardens.

Later visit Umaid Bhawan Palace and explore the historic and cultural attractions of Jodhpur. You can also enjoy shopping for handicrafts, textiles and traditional Rajasthani products.

Overnight: Jodhpur

Day 04 – Jodhpur Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. Depending on your departure schedule, you can enjoy some free time for shopping or local exploration.

Our driver will drop you at Jodhpur Airport, railway station or your preferred departure point for your onward journey.

Tour Ends with Wonderful Memories of Rajasthan.
