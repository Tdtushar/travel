About This Yatra

This extended Char Dham package combines the four sacred Himalayan shrines of Uttarakhand with a dedicated Rishikesh experience.

The traditional pilgrimage sequence is Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath. Rishikesh is added to the beginning and end of the journey to create a more complete spiritual Uttarakhand experience.

The itinerary is designed with additional rest time and can be customised according to group size, travel dates, hotel category and vehicle.

  • Rishikesh Ganga Aarti experience
  • All four Char Dham shrines
  • Private vehicle travel
  • Comfortable en-route accommodation
  • Family and group friendly planning

The Four Sacred Dhams

Four major pilgrimage destinations of Uttarakhand

🌊

Yamunotri

Sacred shrine dedicated to Goddess Yamuna, approached from the Janki Chatti side.

💧

Gangotri

Revered temple dedicated to Goddess Ganga in the Himalayan region of Uttarkashi.

🕉️

Kedarnath

Sacred Lord Shiva shrine located in the high Himalayas of Rudraprayag district.

🙏

Badrinath

Sacred Badrinarayan Temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu in the Alaknanda valley.

Rishikesh Experience

Begin and conclude your pilgrimage beside the Ganga

🌊 Rishikesh – Gateway to the Himalayas

Enjoy a peaceful spiritual experience in Rishikesh with visits to the Ganga ghats, temples and local attractions. Depending on your arrival and departure timings, the itinerary can include Ganga Aarti, Triveni Ghat, Ram Jhula and Laxman Jhula area.

Yatra Route

Complete Char Dham circuit with Rishikesh

Delhi → Rishikesh → Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath → Rishikesh → Delhi

Detailed 13 Days Itinerary

Relaxed Char Dham + Rishikesh travel plan

Day 01 – Delhi → Rishikesh

Depart from Delhi and drive towards Rishikesh. On arrival, check in at the hotel and relax.

In the evening, visit the Ganga riverfront and experience the spiritual atmosphere of Rishikesh. Ganga Aarti can be planned according to timings.

Overnight: Rishikesh

Day 02 – Rishikesh → Barkot

After breakfast, begin the Himalayan journey towards Barkot through the scenic mountain roads.

En-route enjoy views of the Himalayan valleys and the Yamuna region.

Overnight: Barkot

Day 03 – Barkot → Janki Chatti → Yamunotri → Barkot

Early morning proceed towards Janki Chatti. From here begin the pilgrimage towards Yamunotri.

Complete darshan and prayers at Yamunotri Temple. Return towards Janki Chatti and drive back to Barkot.

Overnight: Barkot

Day 04 – Barkot → Uttarkashi

After breakfast, drive towards Uttarkashi. Check in at the hotel and relax.

If time permits, visit the famous Vishwanath Temple and explore the local market.

Overnight: Uttarkashi

Day 05 – Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Uttarkashi

Early morning departure for Gangotri through the beautiful Himalayan landscape.

Visit Gangotri Temple and spend time near the sacred Bhagirathi/Ganga river.

Return to Uttarkashi by evening.

Overnight: Uttarkashi

Day 06 – Uttarkashi → Guptkashi / Sitapur

After breakfast, begin the long mountain journey towards Guptkashi or Sitapur.

The route passes through scenic valleys and mountain towns. Check in and rest on arrival.

Overnight: Guptkashi / Sitapur

Day 07 – Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Early morning transfer towards Sonprayag and the designated Kedarnath access point.

Continue towards Gaurikund and begin the pilgrimage to Kedarnath according to current route and local arrangements.

Reach Kedarnath and attend darshan according to temple timings and local conditions.

Overnight: Kedarnath

Day 08 – Kedarnath → Sonprayag → Guptkashi

Morning darshan and prayers at Kedarnath Temple. After darshan, begin the return journey towards Gaurikund/Sonprayag.

Continue by road towards Guptkashi or Sitapur and relax at the hotel.

Overnight: Guptkashi / Sitapur

Day 09 – Guptkashi → Badrinath

After breakfast, drive towards Badrinath through the scenic Alaknanda valley.

En-route pass through important Himalayan towns and viewpoints before reaching Badrinath.

Check in and, subject to temple timings, plan evening darshan.

Overnight: Badrinath

Day 10 – Badrinath Darshan & Local Sightseeing

Early morning visit to Badrinath Temple for darshan and prayers.

Later explore nearby permitted attractions such as Mana village and other local points, depending on weather and local restrictions.

Overnight: Badrinath

Day 11 – Badrinath → Rudraprayag / Srinagar

After breakfast, start the return journey through the Alaknanda valley.

En-route enjoy the Himalayan scenery and reach Rudraprayag or Srinagar for an overnight stay.

Overnight: Rudraprayag / Srinagar

Day 12 – Rudraprayag / Srinagar → Rishikesh

After breakfast, continue towards Rishikesh.

On arrival, check in and relax. Evening can be kept free for Ganga Aarti, riverside walk and local sightseeing.

Overnight: Rishikesh

Day 13 – Rishikesh → Delhi

After breakfast, depart from Rishikesh for Delhi. The journey concludes with wonderful memories of the Char Dham pilgrimage and Rishikesh.

Tour Ends: Delhi

Important Yatra Information

Prepare properly for the Himalayan pilgrimage

Uttarakhand's Char Dham shrines are high-altitude pilgrimage destinations and the traditional route proceeds from Yamunotri to Gangotri, then Kedarnath and finally Badrinath.

Mountain travel times can vary because of weather, road conditions, traffic, local restrictions and pilgrimage-season arrangements.

Yamunotri and Kedarnath involve substantial pilgrimage walking/trekking sections. Pony, palki and other permitted assistance options may be available subject to local rules and conditions.

⚠ Important

Yatra registration, temple timings, road conditions, weather, trek routes and local transport arrangements can change. Always follow the latest instructions issued by Uttarakhand authorities before and during the journey.
