Tour Overview

Golden Triangle, spiritual Rajasthan and the City of Lakes

Discover India's Heritage & Spiritual Rajasthan

This 09 Nights / 10 Days journey combines the famous Golden Triangle of Delhi, Agra and Jaipur with the spiritual town of Pushkar, historic Ajmer and the romantic city of Udaipur.

Experience India's Mughal heritage in Delhi and Agra, the royal Rajput culture of Jaipur, the sacred atmosphere of Pushkar and Ajmer, and the beautiful lakes and palaces of Udaipur.

With private transportation and a flexible itinerary, the tour is ideal for couples, families and travellers looking for a comfortable introduction to North India and Rajasthan.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

09 Nights / 10 Days

Day 01 – Arrival in Delhi

Arrive at Delhi airport where our representative will meet you and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in and refreshment, enjoy some free time. In the evening, explore the lively markets and streets of Old Delhi.

Day 02 – Delhi Sightseeing

After breakfast, enjoy a full day of sightseeing in Delhi. Visit India Gate, Red Fort, Qutub Minar, President House, Lotus Temple and other important attractions. Evening free for shopping and local exploration.

Day 03 – Delhi to Agra

After breakfast, drive to Agra. On arrival, check into your hotel and relax. Later explore Agra's local markets and handicraft shops. Overnight stay in Agra.

Day 04 – Agra Sightseeing

Visit the magnificent Taj Mahal in the morning. Later explore Agra Fort and other important Mughal heritage sites such as Itmad-ud-Daulah. Evening free for local shopping.

Day 05 – Agra to Jaipur via Fatehpur Sikri

After breakfast, drive towards Jaipur. En-route visit the historic city of Fatehpur Sikri and explore its impressive Mughal architecture. Continue to Jaipur and check into your hotel.

Day 06 – Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore Amber Fort, Jal Mahal, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and other attractions of Jaipur. In the evening, enjoy shopping in the colourful bazaars of the Pink City.

Day 07 – Jaipur to Pushkar

After breakfast, drive to Pushkar. Check into your hotel and explore the sacred town. Visit Brahma Temple, Pushkar Lake and other important temples. Evening free to explore the local market.

Day 08 – Pushkar to Ajmer to Udaipur

After breakfast, travel towards Udaipur with a stop in Ajmer. Visit the famous Ajmer Sharif Dargah and explore the historic surroundings. Continue your journey to Udaipur and check into your hotel.

Day 09 – Udaipur Sightseeing

Enjoy a full day exploring Udaipur. Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari, Jag Mandir and other attractions. In the evening, enjoy the beautiful atmosphere around Lake Pichola and explore local markets.

Day 10 – Udaipur to Delhi / Departure

After breakfast, begin your return journey according to your onward travel arrangements. Transfer to Delhi airport for your departure flight. Your Golden Triangle with Ajmer, Pushkar and Udaipur journey concludes with wonderful memories.
