Golden Triangle & Rajasthan Tour

A long-format journey combining Delhi, Agra, Jaipur and the most celebrated heritage destinations of Rajasthan.

Delhi → Mandawa → Bikaner → Jaisalmer → Jodhpur → Udaipur → Pushkar → Jaipur → Ranthambore → Agra

Tour Overview

Experience the magnificent heritage of North India on this carefully planned Golden Triangle and Rajasthan journey. Starting from Delhi, the tour takes you through colourful towns, historic forts, desert landscapes, royal palaces, lakes and wildlife destinations before finishing in Agra.

From the painted havelis of Mandawa and the desert character of Jaisalmer to the blue city of Jodhpur and romantic lakes of Udaipur, every destination offers a different side of Rajasthan.

The journey also includes Jaipur, Pushkar and Ranthambore, followed by Agra and the iconic Taj Mahal. The itinerary can be modified according to your travel dates, hotel preference and sightseeing interests.

Why Take This Journey?

This route is ideal for travellers who want more than a short Golden Triangle holiday and wish to explore Rajasthan at a comfortable pace with a private vehicle and driver.

Tour Highlights

  • Delhi: Red Fort, India Gate & Qutub Minar
  • Mandawa: Heritage Havelis
  • Bikaner: Junagarh Fort
  • Jaisalmer: Golden Fort & Desert
  • Jodhpur: Mehrangarh Fort
  • Udaipur: City Palace & Lake Pichola
  • Pushkar: Brahma Temple & Lake
  • Jaipur: Amber Fort & Hawa Mahal
  • Ranthambore: Wildlife Safari
  • Agra: Taj Mahal & Agra Fort

Day-by-Day Itinerary

A suggested 16-day route. We can customize the itinerary according to your travel dates and interests.

Day 01: Arrival in Delhi

Arrival at Delhi airport or railway station. Meet our representative and transfer to your hotel. Depending on arrival time, enjoy a relaxed evening or an optional Delhi experience. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 02: Delhi Sightseeing

Explore the highlights of Old and New Delhi including Red Fort, Jama Masjid area, India Gate, Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple and other important landmarks. Evening at leisure.

Day 03: Delhi to Mandawa

Drive towards Mandawa in the Shekhawati region. After check-in, explore the beautifully painted havelis and historic streets. Enjoy the unique architecture and local atmosphere.

Day 04: Mandawa to Bikaner

Continue towards Bikaner. Visit the impressive Junagarh Fort and explore the traditional markets of this historic desert city.

Day 05: Bikaner to Jaisalmer

Drive through the desert landscape towards Jaisalmer. Check into your hotel and relax after the journey.

Day 06: Jaisalmer Sightseeing

Explore Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli, Jain temples and Gadisar Lake. Later enjoy an optional desert experience with camel ride and sunset views.

Day 07: Jaisalmer to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur, the famous Blue City. After arrival, explore the local market and old city lanes.

Day 08: Jodhpur to Udaipur

Visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada in the morning. Continue towards Udaipur. En-route, an optional visit to Ranakpur Jain Temple can be included.

Day 09: Udaipur Sightseeing

Explore City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and other attractions. In the evening, enjoy an optional boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 10: Udaipur to Pushkar

Drive towards Pushkar. Visit Pushkar Lake, Brahma Temple and the colourful streets around the sacred ghats.

Day 11: Pushkar to Jaipur

Continue to Jaipur, Rajasthan's famous Pink City. Check into your hotel and spend the evening exploring local markets or enjoying a traditional cultural dinner.

Day 12: Jaipur Sightseeing

Full-day Jaipur sightseeing including Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal and colourful local markets.

Day 13: Jaipur to Ranthambore

Drive to Ranthambore National Park. Check into your jungle resort and relax amidst the natural surroundings.

Day 14: Ranthambore Safari

Enjoy an optional morning or afternoon jeep/canter safari in Ranthambore National Park. The park is known for its diverse wildlife and birdlife.

Day 15: Ranthambore to Agra

Drive towards Agra. After check-in, visit the magnificent Taj Mahal and explore Agra Fort depending on available time.

Day 16: Agra to Delhi & Departure

After breakfast, drive back to Delhi. Transfer to the airport or railway station for your onward journey. Your Golden Triangle with Rajasthan Cities tour ends with wonderful memories.
