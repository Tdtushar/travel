Premium Char Dham Yatra

The Premium Char Dham Yatra is designed for travellers who want to experience the sacred Char Dham circuit with more comfort, better time management and a less rushed journey.

The traditional pilgrimage route covers Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. Uttarakhand Tourism describes this as the traditional clockwise sequence of the Char Dham pilgrimage.

Instead of trying to complete the journey in a tightly packed schedule, this premium plan provides additional rest and buffer time at important stages of the journey.

The package can be customised according to the age of travellers, group size, hotel category, vehicle preference and trekking requirements.

Premium Package Highlights

  • 12 Nights / 13 Days itinerary
  • Private vehicle for the road journey
  • Comfort-focused hotel stays
  • Extra rest and acclimatisation time
  • Yamunotri & Gangotri Darshan
  • Kedarnath Darshan
  • Badrinath Darshan
  • Dedicated trip coordination
  • Flexible meal and stay options
  • Family and senior-friendly planning

The Four Sacred Dhams

The pilgrimage traditionally begins at Yamunotri, continues to Gangotri, then Kedarnath and finally Badrinath.

Yamunotri Dham

Dedicated to Goddess Yamuna and located in the Himalayan valley near the source of the Yamuna.

Gangotri Dham

The sacred shrine dedicated to Goddess Ganga and situated along the Bhagirathi River.

Kedarnath Dham

The high-altitude shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva in the Garhwal Himalayas.

Badrinath Dham

The sacred Badrinarayan shrine dedicated to Lord Vishnu, located at around 3,100 metres.

12 Nights / 13 Days Detailed Itinerary

A relaxed premium itinerary designed to give pilgrims time for darshan, rest and mountain travel.

Day 01 — Delhi → Haridwar

Pickup from Delhi and comfortable drive towards Haridwar. On arrival, check into the hotel and relax. In the evening, visit Har Ki Pauri and attend Ganga Aarti, subject to timing and local conditions. Overnight stay in Haridwar.

Day 02 — Haridwar → Barkot

After breakfast, proceed towards Barkot through the scenic mountain route. En-route enjoy views of the Himalayan foothills. Arrive at Barkot, check into the hotel and relax for the evening. Overnight stay in Barkot.

Day 03 — Barkot → Janki Chatti → Yamunotri → Barkot

Early morning proceed towards Janki Chatti. From the roadhead, continue towards Yamunotri using the available walking, pony or palanquin options as required. Visit Yamunotri Temple and return towards Barkot after darshan. Overnight stay in Barkot.

Day 04 — Barkot → Uttarkashi

After breakfast, drive towards Uttarkashi. On arrival, check into the hotel and relax. Visit the local Vishwanath Temple area if time permits. The day is intentionally kept lighter for rest before the next mountain journey. Overnight stay in Uttarkashi.

Day 05 — Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Uttarkashi

Early morning drive towards Gangotri through the beautiful Bhagirathi valley. Complete Gangotri Darshan and spend time around the temple and river area. Return to Uttarkashi for a comfortable overnight stay.

Day 06 — Uttarkashi → Harsil

After breakfast, proceed towards Harsil, one of the scenic mountain locations of the route. Enjoy the peaceful surroundings, mountain views and riverside atmosphere. Premium travellers can use this day as a relaxation and photography break. Overnight stay in Harsil.

Day 07 — Harsil → Guptkashi

After breakfast, begin the long but scenic drive towards Guptkashi. The journey passes through mountain valleys, rivers and several Himalayan settlements. Arrive at Guptkashi, check in and rest. Overnight stay in Guptkashi.

Day 08 — Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Proceed towards Sonprayag early in the morning. From the designated transport point, continue towards Kedarnath using the available travel options. Complete Kedarnath Darshan and spend the night near Kedarnath, subject to accommodation availability and local operating conditions.

Day 09 — Kedarnath → Sonprayag → Guptkashi

After morning darshan and breakfast, begin the return journey from Kedarnath towards the lower transport point. Continue to Guptkashi and check into the hotel. The evening is kept free for complete rest after the Kedarnath journey. Overnight stay in Guptkashi.

Day 10 — Guptkashi → Badrinath

After breakfast, drive towards Badrinath through the spectacular Alaknanda valley. On arrival, check into the hotel and rest. If timing and temple operations permit, proceed for Badrinath Darshan in the evening. Overnight stay in Badrinath.

Day 11 — Badrinath Darshan & Local Sightseeing

Early morning Badrinath Darshan. Later visit nearby spiritual and scenic places such as Mana village, subject to local conditions and available time. Return to the hotel and enjoy a relaxed evening in Badrinath. Overnight stay in Badrinath.

Day 12 — Badrinath → Rudraprayag / Srinagar

After breakfast, begin the return journey through the Alaknanda valley. Stop at suitable viewpoints and riverside locations during the journey. Reach Rudraprayag or Srinagar for a comfortable overnight stay.

Day 13 — Rudraprayag / Srinagar → Haridwar → Delhi

After breakfast, drive towards Haridwar. Depending on the departure schedule, continue onward to Delhi. The Premium Char Dham Yatra concludes with drop-off in Delhi.

Important Char Dham Information

Before Booking Your Yatra

Char Dham is a high-altitude Himalayan pilgrimage. The shrines are seasonal and generally remain closed during the winter period because of snowfall.
